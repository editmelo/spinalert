Motion gets noticed. Reflection gets seen.

Approved by the FHWA in 2009, Spin Alert adds an extra degree of attention to any traffic control device. It works in daylight, at night and in bad weather such as rain, sleet, snow, fog and smoke, which is when drivers need the extra warning most.

Wind turns the rotor

Passing traffic and natural breezes keep the vanes turning. The marker needs no power source.

Motion draws the eye

People notice movement in their peripheral vision before they notice a fixed shape. The spinning vanes help drivers spot the device sooner.

Sheeting returns headlights

High-intensity reflective sheeting on each vane reflects headlights back toward the driver, so the motion is still visible after dark.

In use on roads and work zones

Spin Alert fits the devices agencies already put out: channelizing barrels, barricades, crash cushions, barrier walls and warning and regulatory signs.

Built for the field, not the maintenance schedule

Wind-powered operation

Powered entirely by wind for continuous operation. There are no batteries to replace and no external power to run to the work zone.

Better hazard visibility

Its spinning motion makes drivers notice hazards and important signs sooner, giving them more time to slow down or change lanes.

Sustainable energy design

Wind is its only energy source, so there are no electrical connections and no spent batteries to throw away. That makes it a simple choice for agencies with sustainability goals.

High-intensity reflective sheeting

High-intensity reflective sheeting combined with spinning motion makes a hazard warning device that stays highly visible in both daytime and nighttime conditions.

Two sizes, a range of colors

Both sizes weigh less than 2½ lb. 3M Diamond Grade fluorescent yellow and fluorescent orange are the most common sheeting colors, and other reflective colors are available.

6″ × 12″
